# Research merge: three seats, one candidate list

Sources: `research-sol.md` (gpt-6.1-sol), `research-grok.md` (cursor grok-4.7 fast), `research-muse.md` (muse-wonder-full-1.0), all in this folder. Each seat screened about 40 to 60 sites independently. Captures are in `shots/<seat>/<slug>/`. Many captures are partial because the rs01 coordinator capped browser use mid-run. Several sites are judged from HTML only.

## Where the seats agree

Every seat reached these independently.

1. **Base archetype is the editorial column.** Around a 640px measure, a 16px body, one large name, sections as text rows. All three name Paco, Emil and Jakub. All three say /lab/profile is closest and should be the base.
2. **A project row is the name plus one sentence of outcome**, optionally an icon and a date. Rows, not tiles with adjectives.
3. **Product imagery one at a time.** Samuel Kraft's pattern: a text intro, then one wide media card per flagship project with a caption. The bento's three-up screenshots are the right assets in the wrong shape.
4. **Exactly one memorable detail, and it should belong to Oskar.** The clock, the theme switch, the variant dock and the stats band currently compete for it.
5. **A dated Now or Today block** (Emil, Paco). The current "Working on now" badges are 80% of it.
6. **Depth beyond the homepage.** Two or three case studies (Hydra and its Rust rebuild, the Netflix bot with the Webby, Groovy) that say what Oskar personally owned and decided. Writing comes later and never as an empty route.
7. **A clear next step for clients.** What to hire him for, and a closing contact block like Story's "Have something to build?". Never invent an availability date.
8. **Cut the skills chip wall.** Tools belong inside sentences or per role.
9. **Story's headline is the best line on the site.** "I build software for millions of Discord servers, and have since I was 16."
10. **Refuse gates and posters.** Henry Heffernan's START screen, Bruno Simon's drive-to-read world and canvas-only pages fail a client with four minutes.

## Where the seats disagree

- **The stats band.** Muse: four counters as a hero is SaaS-landing grammar, so attach each number to its project row. Grok: one band of four, then stop. Sol: it repeats the biography's scale argument.
- **What the memorable detail is.** Candidates: the Story year ruler (Grok), Lee Robinson's Default/Long bio toggle (Grok, Muse), a ⌘K palette (Muse for, Sol against for a site with a handful of destinations), a live Hydra server count (Muse, Sol cautious), a small Discord slash-command playground (Sol, Muse), Dennis-style hover image previews on work rows (Muse), Antfu's inline project pills (Muse).
- **Brittany Chiang.** Sol and Muse put her in their top 3 for the sticky identity and dated rows. Muse warns the layout is the most-forked portfolio template and reads as junior if copied.
- **Where Story goes.** A homepage chapter (Grok, Muse partly) or an optional /story or /about route (Sol, Muse).
